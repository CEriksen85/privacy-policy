Privacy Policy for SyncVia
Last updated: 23 July 2026

SyncVia is an app for shared planning, booking and resource overview for families and small groups.

This privacy policy applies to SyncVia on Android and iOS.

Information We Collect

SyncVia may collect and process the following information:

- Name, email address and account information used for login
- User ID used to identify the user account inside the app
- Family or group information
- Booking information, such as selected resource, date, time, purpose and user name
- Subscription status and purchase information from Google Play, Apple App Store and RevenueCat
- Technical data needed for authentication, security and app functionality

How We Use Information

We use the information to:

- Let users create and manage a family or group
- Let users create, view and manage bookings
- Manage access to Free, Premium and Premium Plus features
- Process and verify subscriptions through Google Play, Apple App Store and RevenueCat
- Provide account, booking and resource functionality
- Provide support if users contact us

Data Storage

User data may be stored using Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

Subscription and purchase status may be processed by RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple App Store.

Third-Party Services

SyncVia may use third-party services, including:

- Firebase
- Google Play services
- Apple App Store services
- RevenueCat

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

SyncVia does not sell personal data.

SyncVia does not use personal data for third-party advertising or tracking.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers only when needed to operate the app, provide subscriptions, authentication, database storage or technical functionality.

Data Deletion

Users can delete their account inside the app where account deletion is available.

When an account is deleted, the user account and associated personal account data are deleted. Some booking history may be retained in anonymized form where needed for family or group statistics, but personal identifiers such as email address and user ID should be removed from that history.

Users may also contact the developer for questions about data deletion.

Subscription cancellation must be handled through Google Play subscriptions or Apple App Store subscriptions, depending on where the subscription was purchased.

Children

SyncVia is intended for general family and group use. The app does not knowingly collect more personal information than needed to provide the service.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data and use Firebase and platform services for authentication and storage.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be available at the privacy policy URL provided for the app.
